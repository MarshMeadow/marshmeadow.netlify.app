Privacy Notes

This is a personal portfolio and bio website. I try to keep it lightweight and privacy-friendly.

No cookies or tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party scripts that follow you around the web. The only external resources loaded are Google Fonts and data fetched directly from GitHub's public API.

What is stored locally

Some features save small preferences in your browser's localStorage. This is only used for:

  • Remembering that you solved the puzzle so you don't have to solve it again on every link.
  • Theme, size, layout, and visibility settings you choose in the settings menu.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

External links

Some links go to external sites such as GitHub, Ko-fi, Patreon, and project websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Most external links open in a new tab and are marked where appropriate.

Donations

Donation links go through Ko-fi and Patreon. I do not collect or see any payment information directly on this site.

Content use

By viewing this site you agree not to copy, redistribute, scrape, or abuse any content, images, code, or personal information found on these pages. See the full notice / disclaimer for more details.
