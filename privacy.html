Privacy Notes

This is a personal portfolio and bio website. I try to keep it lightweight and privacy-friendly.

No cookies or tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party scripts that follow you around the web. The only external resources loaded are Google Fonts and data fetched directly from GitHub's public API. If you turn on the optional background music, a YouTube player is loaded.

What is stored locally

Some features save small preferences in your browser's localStorage / sessionStorage. This is only used for:

  • Remembering that you passed the quick human check and acknowledged the notice.
  • Your settings: falling petals, reduced motion, and background music on/off, volume and link.
  • A short-lived cache of my public GitHub repos so the page doesn't hit GitHub's rate limit.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

External links

Some links go to external sites such as GitHub, Ko-fi, Patreon, and project websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. External links open in a new tab.

Donations

Donation links go through Ko-fi and Patreon. I do not collect or see any payment information directly on this site.
